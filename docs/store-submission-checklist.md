# Intrinsic Store Submission Checklist

## Product Readiness

- [ ] Onboarding includes educational-only disclaimer and user acknowledgment.
- [ ] Settings includes persistent disclaimer references.
- [ ] Privacy controls available: data export and account deletion flow.
- [ ] Freemium limits and Pro entitlements enforced by backend.
- [ ] RevenueCat products configured for US and India pricing.

## iOS App Store (iOS-first)

- [ ] App metadata, subtitle, keywords, support URL, and privacy policy URL set.
- [ ] Screenshots for iPhone sizes uploaded.
- [ ] In-app purchase subscriptions configured and reviewed.
- [ ] App Privacy details submitted (data collection + usage purposes).
- [ ] Guideline compliance checked: no financial advice language.

## Android Play Store

- [ ] Play listing details and screenshots uploaded.
- [ ] Subscription products configured and mapped.
- [ ] Data safety form completed.
- [ ] Content rating submitted.

## Legal & Policy

- [ ] Privacy Policy reviewed by counsel and published.
- [ ] Terms of Service reviewed by counsel and published.
- [ ] Risk disclosure language validated across onboarding/settings/website.

## Technical Release

- [ ] API production env vars set (Firebase, database, Redis, webhooks).
- [ ] Database migrations applied.
- [ ] CI passing (tests/build/typecheck).
- [ ] Crash/error monitoring configured.
