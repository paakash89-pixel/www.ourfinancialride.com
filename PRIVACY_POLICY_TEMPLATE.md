# Privacy Policy Template - Intrinsic

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Intrinsic ("we", "our", "us") provides educational financial analysis and behavioral coaching tools.

## 1. Educational Nature

Intrinsic is not an investment advisory service and does not provide personalized buy/sell recommendations.

## 2. Information We Collect

- Account identifiers (UID, email where available)
- Profile settings (region, currency, privacy preferences)
- PCC and discipline event records
- AI chat messages (only when not in private mode and memory is enabled)
- Subscription/entitlement records from billing providers
- Usage counters for plan limits

## 3. How We Use Information

- Operate discipline workflows (Stress Mode, PCC, Sell Friction, Regret Ledger)
- Enforce plan limits and entitlement gates
- Provide account support and reliability monitoring
- Comply with legal obligations

## 4. AI Privacy Controls

Users can:
- Enable Private Mode (no chat storage)
- Delete chat history
- Opt in/out of AI memory
- Export account data

## 5. Sharing and Processors

We share data only with service providers required to run the product (hosting, authentication, billing, infrastructure, and AI provider where enabled).

## 6. Retention

We retain data while needed for service operation, legal obligations, and security, unless deleted through user controls.

## 7. Security

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to risk, including access controls and transport security.

## 8. User Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion, and export of their personal data.
