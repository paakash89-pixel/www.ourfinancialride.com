Stock tips feel productive.

They are usually emotional entertainment.

Tip-driven investing creates a dangerous cycle:

Noise.
Urgency.
Action.
Regret.

The antidote is not more research.

It is a better operating system.

Our stock-tip detox rule was simple:

If it is not in our written policy, we do not act.

No exceptions during market euphoria.
No exceptions during market fear.

Replace tip-checking with a fixed process:

1) Auto-invest on schedule.
2) Rebalance only by policy.
3) Review once per quarter.

Most families do not need better forecasts.

They need fewer unplanned decisions.

Peace in investing is a process design problem.
